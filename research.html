Research

Research Interests

  • I specialize in structural coding theory, with a focus on linear codes over finite fields and finite rings — investigating their algebraic properties and structures.
  • My current research centers on constructing new record-breaking quantum error-correcting codes by studying families of linear codes. Recently, we obtained new record-breaking quantum codes from quasi-cyclic codes and group ring codes.
  • I also explore a range of applications of coding theory, including DNA codes, code-based cryptography, and other structural applications.
  • I am additionally interested in coding theory in connection with finite geometry, algebraic geometry, and topological codes. If any of these directions interest you and you'd like to discuss a potential project, I'd be happy to talk.
