Open Positions

PhD and Postdoctoral Researchers

I am always interested in collaborating with highly motivated students and researchers who are passionate about algebraic coding theory and its applications to quantum error-correcting codes and DNA coding. If your research interests align with these areas, I would be delighted to hear from you.

I am also interested in:

  • Algebraic Geometry Codes
  • Code-Based Cryptography

PhD Opportunities

I welcome applications from motivated students with a strong mathematical background and a genuine interest in algebraic coding theory and its applications.

Core requirements — applicants should have a solid foundation in:

  • Abstract Algebra (Group Theory, Ring Theory, Module Theory and Field Theory)
  • Linear Algebra

Beyond that, I'm always glad to hear from students already working in — or keen to learn — one or more of the following areas:

  • Algebraic Geometry
  • Algebraic Topology (Homology Theory)
  • Combinatorics (Graph Theory)

Prior research experience is welcome but not required.

Postdoctoral Opportunities

I welcome inquiries from researchers with a strong publication record and demonstrated research potential. Applicants should have expertise in algebraic coding theory or one or more of the following related areas:

  • Quantum Error-Correcting Codes
  • Code-Based Cryptography
  • Algebraic Geometry Codes

Researchers working in closely related areas are also encouraged to contact me.

How to Apply

If you are interested in working with me, please send an email containing the following materials.

PhD applicants

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Details of any research experience, projects, or publications (if applicable)

Postdoctoral applicants

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • A brief research statement describing your research interests
  • A complete list of publications
  • A short summary of your previous research and future research plans
