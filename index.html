Assistant Professor · Mathematics

Tushar Bag

Department of Mathematics, SRM University AP, Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, India

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at SRM University-AP. My research lies in algebraic coding theory, with a primary focus on constructing quantum error-correcting codes.

Prior to joining SRM University-AP, I held postdoctoral research positions at École Normale Supérieure de Lyon (France), Carleton University (Canada), and United Arab Emirates University. I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology Patna and my M.Sc. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur.
